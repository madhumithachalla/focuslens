    With no schedules, blocking is always on. With schedules, blocking applies only inside active windows. Windows can cross midnight (e.g. 22:00 to 06:00).

      While a schedule window or focus session is active, settings cannot be edited or paused, and emergency passes are switched off.

      Question gate

      Before a blocked site opens you answer questions. They get harder the more often you try to get in during a day, and within a gate two right answers in a row raise the level.
